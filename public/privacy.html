Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Knowura AI is built and operated by Uday Singh, an individual based in India, who is the "data controller" / "data fiduciary" for the personal data described here. This page explains what data Knowura handles, why, who gets it, and what control you have. Short version: no ads, no analytics or tracking cookies, and we don't sell your data.

1. What we handle, and why

  • Your messages. What you type or say is sent to an AI provider to generate replies (purpose: giving you the service). In guest mode chats are saved only on your device; if you're signed in they're also saved to your account (see below).
  • Voice audio. In Live Voice mode, short audio clips are sent to Groq to be transcribed, and reply text is sent to Groq to be turned into speech. We don't keep the audio ourselves and don't create voiceprints or identify you from your voice.
  • Phone wake word (optional, off by default). If you choose "Chat & Voice" for the wake word on a phone, Knowura keeps the microphone on while the page is open and visible. Short bursts of speech (a few seconds) are sent to Groq to be transcribed only to check for "Hey Knowura". We don't keep these clips, and nothing is recorded when the page is in the background. Turn it off in Settings to stop.
  • Web search queries. When a question looks like it needs fresh information ("latest", "news", "who is"…), the question is sent to Tavily to fetch search results.
  • Google account info (only if you sign in). Your Google account ID, name, email address, and profile picture, received through Google Sign-In. We verify your sign-in on our server and use this only to identify your saved data and show your name and picture. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.
  • Memory. If "Remember Conversations" is on, Knowura extracts short facts about you (e.g. subjects you study) and a rolling summary from your chats and includes them in future requests so replies feel personal. It is used only to improve your own replies: never for advertising, profiling for others, or tracking. Turn it off, or use "Forget Remembered Facts", at any time.
  • Android assistant (optional). If you set the Knowura Android app as your phone's digital assistant, your phone's assistant gesture (for example holding the power or home button) opens Knowura's floating panel, which starts in voice mode. While it is open it listens hands-free and sends your speech to Groq to be transcribed and answered, the same way voice calls work, and the microphone stops when you close the panel. The panel uses the account you signed in with in the Knowura app: the Android app creates a random install ID and gives it to the site when it opens, and while you are signed in the site links that ID to your account (and to the assistant model you chose in Settings) on our server, which stores only a hash of the ID. Signing out of the app removes the link. Chats from a signed-in panel are saved to your Knowura account like chats from the app; guest chats stay on your phone for a short time and are handed to the app only when you swipe the panel up into it. Knowura does not read your screen or other apps.
  • Technical data. Our hosting providers (Vercel and Netlify) and Google's sign-in script (which loads when you open Knowura, even if you never sign in) receive basic request data such as your IP address, browser type and timestamps, which is how the internet works and is used for delivery, security and operations.
  • Usage limits. To stop abuse and keep the service affordable, we count how many messages and sign-in attempts come from each account or device. For guests this uses a hashed (pseudonymised) form of your IP address. We don't store the address itself, but a hash can still count as personal data, so we treat it that way. Counters expire automatically (within 24 hours).

2. Why we're allowed to use it (legal basis)

  • To provide the service you asked for (messages, voice, saved chats): necessary to perform our agreement with you.
  • Your consent for optional things: signing in, microphone access, the wake word, and memory. You can withdraw consent at any time (sign out, deny the microphone, turn memory off or delete your data) without affecting what happened before.
  • Legitimate interests in keeping Knowura secure and affordable (abuse limits, security logs), balanced against your rights.
  • Legal obligations where the law requires us to keep or hand over something.

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 we rely on your consent and on the "legitimate uses" the Act allows. We don't make decisions about you based on automated profiling that have legal or similarly significant effects; AI replies are just answers.

3. Where it's stored

  • Guests: chats and memory live in your browser's local storage on your device only.
  • Signed-in users: your chats, memory and basic profile are saved in a private cloud storage bucket (Vercel Blob or Netlify Blobs, depending on which site you're using), keyed to your Google account ID. Each deployment has its own separate storage; data isn't shared between knowura.vercel.app and knowura.netlify.app.
  • On your device: settings (voice mode, wake word, model choice, layout, etc.), your "stay signed in" session, volume, and the choice to dismiss the cookie notice are kept in local storage.

4. Who we share it with

Only the services needed to run Knowura, each acting under its own terms and privacy policy:

  • Groq: AI chat models, speech-to-text (including the phone wake-word clips), and text-to-speech.
  • Tavily: web search.
  • Google: Sign-In (Google Identity Services); on desktop Chrome, the optional wake word, which uses the browser's speech recognition that Google processes.
  • Vercel and Netlify: hosting and storage.
  • Live previews: if you open a preview of AI-written HTML, that page may itself load images, fonts or code libraries from other websites, which then see your IP address. Knowura's own pages host their fonts and code libraries themselves.
  • Authorities or other parties where the law requires it, or to protect our rights and users' safety.

We don't sell your personal data, don't "share" it for cross-context advertising, and don't run analytics or tracking scripts. California residents: that means there is nothing for you to opt out of, and we won't discriminate against you for using your privacy rights.

Google user data. Knowura's use of information received from Google Sign-In will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements: we use it only to provide the sign-in and saved-chats features described here, don't use or transfer it for advertising, and don't allow people to read it except for security, to comply with the law, or with your consent.

5. International transfers

Knowura is run from India, and the providers above (for example Groq, Tavily, Google, Vercel and Netlify) process data in the United States and other countries, so your data is likely to leave your country. We rely on the safeguards those providers offer (such as standard contractual clauses and their own security programmes) and share only what each feature needs. By using Knowura you understand your data may be processed in those places.

6. Cookies and local storage

Knowura doesn't set advertising or analytics cookies. It uses your browser's local storage for the essentials above (settings, guest chats, sign-in session, and remembering that you dismissed the cookie notice). Google's sign-in button may set its own cookies to make signing in work; those are governed by Google's policies. You can clear all of this in your browser settings at any time. Doing so will sign you out and remove guest chats from that device.

7. How long we keep it

  • Chats and memory stay until you delete them (per-chat delete, "Delete All Chats", "Forget Remembered Facts"). For signed-in users, deletions are saved to your cloud copy too. You can also ask us to delete your whole cloud record.
  • Logging out clears the local session and the chats loaded in your browser; your saved cloud data remains for next time you sign in, until you delete it or ask us to.
  • Usage-limit counters expire automatically within 24 hours.
  • Hosting logs and third-party providers keep data according to their own policies. Some may keep it for a limited time for security and abuse prevention even after you delete a chat.

8. Security

Traffic is encrypted over HTTPS. Sign-in is verified on our server, signed session tokens are used to access cloud data, and cloud storage is private. No system is perfectly secure, and AI text you send is processed by third parties, so please don't share passwords, ID numbers, or other sensitive details in chats. If a breach affects your data we will tell you and the authorities where the law requires it.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, India's DPDP Act, or US state laws) you may have the right to access your data, have it corrected or deleted, receive a copy in a portable format, restrict or object to some processing, withdraw consent, nominate someone to exercise your rights, and make a complaint. You can do much of this yourself:

  • Use guest mode to keep everything on your device.
  • Delete chats, forget remembered facts, or turn memory off in the menu.
  • Use Incognito for a chat that isn't saved on your device or account and isn't added to memory. Your messages still go to the AI provider to be answered, and hosting providers may keep basic technical logs; closing or refreshing the page discards the chat.
  • Deny microphone access, mute or end voice calls, and turn the wake word off in Settings (this stops the always-on microphone on phones).
  • For a copy of your saved data, to correct it, or to have your account's cloud data fully deleted, contact us (below). We may need to confirm it's you first, and we'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we handled your request, tell us first (email above) so we can fix it. You also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority (in the EU/UK, your national regulator; in India, the Data Protection Board of India once it is operating).

10. Children and teens

Knowura isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. People under 18 may use it only with a parent or guardian's permission (see the Terms). We don't show ads, don't track children across the web, and don't use anyone's data for advertising. Parents and guardians can contact us to see, correct or delete a child's data, or to withdraw their consent. If you think a child under 13 has signed in, contact us and we'll delete the data promptly.

11. Changes

If this policy changes we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant. If you use Knowura after an update, the new version applies; where the law requires fresh consent for a change, we'll ask for it.
