6. Voice features

Live Voice mode asks for microphone access. Your speech is recorded in short clips and sent to a speech-to-text provider to be transcribed; replies may be spoken back using a text-to-speech provider. The optional "Hey Knowura" wake word works in two ways: on desktop browsers it uses the browser's built-in speech recognition (in Chrome this is processed by Google); on phones, if you turn on "Chat & Voice", the microphone stays on while Knowura is open and short speech clips are sent to our speech provider to listen for the phrase. You can mute, end the call, or turn the wake word off at any time in Settings. Only record yourself, or people who know and agree to it. In the Android app you can also choose Knowura as your phone's digital assistant, which opens a floating panel over your current app, starting in voice mode. It works like the main chat, voice and study features, can sign in with your Google account so chats sync to the app, and doesn't read your screen.
