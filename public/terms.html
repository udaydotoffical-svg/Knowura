1. What Knowura is

Knowura is an education-focused AI assistant. You can chat by text or voice, keep multiple chats, and (optionally) sign in with Google so your chats and memory follow your account. Replies are generated by third-party AI models and may use web search results.

2. Who can use it

3. Accounts and guest mode

  • Guest mode needs no account. Your chats and memory stay on your device only.
  • Google sign-in is optional. It lets us save your chats and memory to your account so they're available across devices.
  • You're responsible for activity on your account and for keeping your device secure. "Stay signed in" keeps you signed in on that device until you log out — don't use it on a shared computer.

6. Voice features

Live Voice mode asks for microphone access. Your speech is recorded in short clips and sent to a speech-to-text provider to be transcribed; replies may be spoken back using a text-to-speech provider. The optional "Hey Knowura" wake word uses your browser's built-in speech recognition (in Chrome this is processed by Google). You can mute, end the call, or turn the wake word off at any time in Settings.
