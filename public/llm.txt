# Knowura AI

> Knowura AI is a free, education-first AI study helper made by Uday Singh, a student who builds robotics, web apps and AI tools. You can chat by text or talk by voice, get anything explained until it clicks, and turn a topic into a quiz or flashcards in seconds.

Knowura runs in the browser at https://knowura.vercel.app/ and also as an Android app (with a pull-up assistant panel) and a Windows desktop app (tray popup with hotkeys). Signing in with Google is optional; it syncs chats and memory across devices.

## What it does

- Text chat and hands-free voice conversations, with a choice of AI models and a "think harder" mode.
- Quizzes and flashcards that pop up as interactive study cards.
- Attach photos, PDFs, Word documents, spreadsheets, code and audio to a question.
- Code answers open in a side panel with a live, sandboxed preview for HTML, SVG and Markdown.
- Saved chats, optional memory, an incognito mode and a built-in lo-fi music player.
- Safety checks on messages and pictures before the AI answers.
