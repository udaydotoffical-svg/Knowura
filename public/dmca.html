Copyright & DMCA Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Knowura AI ("Knowura", "we") respects the rights of creators and expects everyone who uses it to do the same. This page explains what you may put into Knowura, how to tell us about suspected copyright infringement, and how we handle those reports under the U.S. Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512) and similar laws.

1. What you may put into Knowura

You can type, paste, or speak text into Knowura, and it may be sent to the AI and speech providers listed in our Privacy Policy. Only submit text and other material that you own, that is in the public domain, or that you have permission or a legal right to use. Don't paste in someone else's copyrighted work (for example whole book chapters, paid course material, or exam papers) and ask Knowura to copy, reproduce, or distribute it. Our Terms and EULA apply.

2. AI-generated replies

Knowura's answers are generated automatically by third-party AI models. They can sometimes resemble existing material. Don't present AI output as your own original work where that isn't allowed (for example in exams or assignments), and check anything you plan to publish. Chats are private to you: we don't publish them.

3. Reporting suspected infringement (DMCA notice)

If you believe material handled by Knowura infringes your copyright, send a written notice that includes all of the following:

  • your physical or electronic signature (or that of a person authorised to act for the copyright owner);
  • a description of the copyrighted work you say was infringed;
  • a description of the material you say is infringing and enough detail for us to locate it (Knowura chats are private, so tell us the account or how the material reached the service, and the approximate date);
  • your name, postal address, telephone number and email address;
  • a statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law; and
  • a statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorised to act for them.

Send notices to the maintainer, Uday Singh, through the project's GitHub repository: github.com/udaydotoffical-svg/Knowura. Please don't post your personal details in a public issue — open a minimal issue titled "DMCA notice" and we'll arrange a private channel, or use the contact listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Notices that are missing the items above may not be actionable. Knowingly making a false claim can make you liable for damages.

3A. Notices under Indian law

Notices from rights holders under Section 52(1)(c) of the Indian Copyright Act should include the same details as above and enough information to show that you own or exclusively license the work and that the material is an infringing copy. When we receive a complete written notice we will disable access to the reported material for 21 days; if you haven't obtained a court order by then, we may restore it.

4. What we do with a valid notice

When we receive a complete notice we will act promptly, which may include removing or disabling access to the reported material, notifying the person who submitted it, and, where appropriate, limiting or ending their access to Knowura.

5. Counter-notice

If your material was removed and you believe that was a mistake or that you have the right to use it, you may send a counter-notice through the same channel containing: your signature; a description of the material and where it was before removal; a statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief it was removed by mistake or misidentification; your name, address and phone number; and consent to the jurisdiction of the court for your address (or, outside the U.S., of the courts of India) and to accept service from the person who sent the original notice. We may forward the counter-notice to the person who complained, and may restore the material within 10–14 business days unless they tell us they have filed a court action.

6. Repeat infringers

We may suspend or end access for people who are repeatedly reported for infringement, in appropriate circumstances.

7. Not legal advice

This page is a plain-language description of how Knowura handles copyright reports. It isn't legal advice.
